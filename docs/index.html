Plinket

A bare-essentials music player for your Navidrome library, made for intentional, album-first listening.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. Now available on the App Store.

  • Albums firstBrowse your library by album, artist, playlist and favorites, or search it.
  • Just playA simple queue with near-gapless playback, shuffle and repeat.
  • Everywhere you listenLock screen, Control Center, media keys and AirPlay.
  • Your serverScrobbles plays and syncs favorites with Navidrome or any Subsonic server.
  • Nothing extraNo discovery, mixes, radio or recommendations.
  • PrivateNo accounts, analytics or tracking. Your music stays between you and your server.
