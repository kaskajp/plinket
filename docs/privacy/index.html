Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Plinket is a music player for your own Navidrome or Subsonic server. There are no Plinket accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. We do not sell your data. Optional problem reports are sent only when you choose to submit them.

What stays on your device

To sign you in, Plinket stores your server address and username on your device, and your password in the system Keychain. It also keeps a cache of your library and cover art, your playback queue, and any plays still waiting to be sent to your server. All of this stays on your device and is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

What goes to your server

For music playback, Plinket talks to the server you sign in to. It sends that server the requests needed to browse and play your music, the songs you play (scrobbles) and the albums and songs you mark as favorites. Your password is never sent; Plinket signs in with a token derived from it. Plinket connects over HTTPS, except to a server on your local network that you enter with an http:// address. What your server does with this information is up to whoever runs it.

Optional problem reports

When you submit Report a Problem, Plinket sends your summary, description, chosen screenshots, app version, operating system and language over HTTPS to a developer-operated relay. The relay forwards the report to the developer’s private Linear issue tracker for support. No music server address, username, password or listening history is added automatically. Screenshots are resized and re-encoded without source photo metadata; review their visible contents and your description for private information before sending.

The relay uses your IP address temporarily in memory to limit abuse and does not save report contents or IP addresses to disk. Your report and screenshots are stored in Linear and are available to the developer for investigation. They are not used for advertising or tracking. You can request deletion through the contact link below; include the report summary and approximate submission time so the developer can locate it.

Local network

If your server is on your local network, iOS and macOS ask for permission to connect to it. Plinket uses that permission only to reach your server.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with Apple in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. Plinket does not send automatic crash reports to the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on GitHub.
